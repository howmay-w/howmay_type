感謝 TypeCon，度過了非常精彩愉快的美國時間！

被字體話題包圍真是幸福，對一切的親切鼓勵對話熱情與狂問問題都無限感激，嗚嗚嗚。

For friends unfamiliar with the phrase — ‘American Time’ (美國時間) is a Taiwanese idiom that usually means ‘spare time,’ but here I mean it literally: time spent in America.

Thank you, TypeCon. You were amazing!
